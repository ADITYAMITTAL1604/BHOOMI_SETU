4. OBJECTIONS RAISED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Objection 1: Inadequate Compensation
   The objector contends that the prevailing circle rate of ₹85 lakh
   per hectare does not reflect actual market transactions in the area,
   which have been recorded at ₹1.1 crore per hectare in recent sales.

Objection 2: Livelihood Impact
   The objector states that sugarcane cultivation on the land is the
   primary source of income for a family of 6 members and requests
   enhanced R&R benefits including employment guarantee.

Objection 3: Water Source
   The bore well on the land serves not only the objector's farm but
   also provides water to 3 adjacent properties. The objector requests
   provision for alternative water supply arrangement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. RESPONSE BY REQUIRING BODY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Representative          : Shri Nitin Bhosale, Land Officer, MSRDC

Response to Objection 1:
   The compensation is computed strictly as per Section 26-28 of the
   Act and includes 100% solatium. Recent sale instances will be
   considered during final award computation.

Response to Objection 2:
   Enhanced R&R entitlements under Schedule II are applicable. One
   family member will be offered employment in the project as per
   Section 31(4).

Response to Objection 3:
   MSRDC undertakes to construct a community bore well within 500m
   radius of the affected properties as a project obligation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. FINDINGS AND ORDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After considering the objections and responses, the following order
is passed:

1. Objection 1 (Compensation): PARTIALLY UPHELD
   - Recent sale data to be examined during Section 23 award
   - Collector to verify 3 comparable sale instances

2. Objection 2 (Livelihood): UPHELD
   - Enhanced R&R benefits to be included in award
   - Employment guarantee letter to be issued by MSRDC

3. Objection 3 (Water Source): UPHELD
   - MSRDC to construct community bore well before possession
   - Timeline: Within 45 days of award declaration

The overall acquisition is found to be necessary for public purpose
and shall proceed subject to the above conditions.
